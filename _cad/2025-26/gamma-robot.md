Gamma was Beargineers' clean-sheet robot rebuild for the FIRST Championship in Houston.

## Design themes

- Smooth exterior packaging to avoid catching on walls or other robots.
- Integrated number plate holder.
- Turreted shooter for scoring independent of robot heading.
- Single motor intake and feeder compromise caused by motor allocation.
- Front-facing camera in a custom housing for interchangeable vision experiments.
