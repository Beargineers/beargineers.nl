## Sponsor Beargineers

We are looking for additional sponsors who want to support hands-on STEM education. Sponsorship helps the team buy robot hardware, electronics, field materials, tools, and travel support for competitions.

Partners can support Beargineers through funding, materials, technical mentoring, manufacturing access, or introductions to engineering professionals.
