Fusion 360 is most useful for FTC when it helps the team make decisions before cutting material.

## What to learn first

- Sketch constraints and dimensions.
- Components versus bodies.
- Assemblies and joints.
- Vendor part imports.
- Clearance checks around motors, belts, wheels, wiring, and game elements.
- Exporting STEP and STL files for manufacturing.

## Beargineers angle

Every CAD model should answer a design question. If it cannot be manufactured, assembled, maintained, or explained, it is not finished.
